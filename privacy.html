This policy explains what personal information Innovation Vision Consulting Group (“IVCG”, “we”) collects through ivconsultinggroup.com, why, and what choices you have. IVCG is a trade name of IVC Blueprint LLC.

Who is responsible

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, company, role, email address and message. If you send the 60-second diagnostic, your three answers are added to the message.
  • Visit statistics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites and does not build a profile of you.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting provider and font service receive your IP address and browser details in order to deliver the page.

The diagnostic itself runs in your browser. Your answers are not sent anywhere unless you choose to send them through the contact form.

Why we use it

  • To answer your inquiry and discuss a possible engagement.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the website is used and improve it.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

  • Formspree receives contact form submissions and forwards them to our email.
  • Google Workspace hosts our email.
  • Cloudflare provides visit statistics.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website, and Google Fonts serves its typefaces.

These providers may process data in the United States. Where the law requires it, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Legal basis (visitors in the European Union and the United Kingdom)

We process contact information to respond to your request before a possible contract, and visit statistics on the basis of our legitimate interest in running and improving the website.

How long we keep it

Inquiries are kept for as long as needed to follow up and, if no engagement follows, for no more than three years after our last exchange.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to its use, or ask for a copy. Email contact@ivconsultinggroup.com. If you are in the European Union, you can also contact your local data protection authority.
