Who we are

One partner for strategy, operations and finance.

Innovation Vision Consulting Group (IVCG) is a Miami-based advisory firm. We work with founders, executives and boards at the moments that shape an organization: launching, scaling, raising capital, merging, restructuring or changing leadership.

Most firms look at one piece of the problem. We connect all three: where the organization is going, how it actually runs, and whether the numbers hold. That integrated view lets us find root causes, not just symptoms, and build plans that work in practice.

Our signature expertise is in sports and live entertainment, industries where audiences are demanding, deadlines don’t move and execution is everything. The same discipline serves growing organizations in any sector.

We work as a long-term strategic partner, not a traditional agency.
