Accessibility

Last updated: October 23, 2026

We want ivconsultinggroup.com to be usable by everyone, and we aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

What we have done

  • Every page works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Text and buttons use strong color contrast.
  • Images, icons and interactive diagrams have text alternatives.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, animations are switched off.
  • The layout adapts to phones, tablets and zoom.

Known limitations

Some decorative animations rely on visual effects. All of the information they illustrate is also available as text on the page.

Feedback

If something is hard to use, tell us at contact@ivconsultinggroup.com and we will help you get the information another way and work on a fix.
