Add it to Gmail

  1. In Gmail, click the gear icon (top right) → See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature → Create new, and name it (for example “IVCG” or “IVCG General”).
  3. Click in the box and paste (Cmd + V on Mac, Ctrl + V on Windows).
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and replies. The general signature goes in the contact@ account (or, if contact@ is an alias in your Gmail, pick the contact@ address in the For dropdown).
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.

If the copy button does nothing, select the signature in the white box with your mouse, copy it, and paste it the same way.
